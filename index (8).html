M04

LV Distribution, Earthing, Protection & Multiple Infeeds

Develop the LV system understanding that is often the largest transition for an HV-focused AP/SAP.

Module method: learn → visualise → connect → apply → test → revisit in another system → demonstrate through evidence.

Learning sequence

LESSON 01

Conductors and return paths

Revisit phase, neutral, protective conductors and exposed conductive parts through the question: where can normal and fault current return?

LESSON 02

Fault families

Distinguish overload, short-circuit and insulation/earth faults and connect each to expected protection behaviour.

LESSON 03

Protection devices

Fuse, circuit breaker, residual protection, insulation monitoring and electronic trip functions in context.

LESSON 04

Earthing arrangements

Compare TT, TN-S, TN-C and IT conceptually, focusing on fault-current paths, neutral-earth relationships and operational implications.

LESSON 05

Sectionalised LV boards

Interpret incomers, bus sections, couplers, outgoing ways, A/B distribution and changed configurations.

LESSON 06

Multiple infeeds and backfeed

Trace every credible source rather than relying on the normal supply path.

LESSON 07

Breaking and withstand capability

Introduce prospective fault current and Icu/Ics/Icw/Icm as equipment-selection and operational-awareness concepts.

LESSON 08

Trip units and selectivity

Develop LSIG, time/current coordination and selective fault clearance conceptually; settings remain study- and equipment-specific.

Module assessment

Micro-checks are embedded inside lessons. Development checks then test diagram recognition, energy-path tracing, explanation and application. Integrated competency is assessed later across module boundaries; completing this page does not establish competence.
