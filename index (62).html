POWERTEC TRAININGPROGRAMME
M08

Commissioning, Temporary Configurations, Load Banks & IST

Develop disciplined configuration awareness when the installed system is being progressively energised, tested or temporarily modified.

Module method: learn → visualise → connect → apply → test → revisit in another system → demonstrate through evidence.

Learning sequence

LESSON 01

Construction to operational state

Differentiate design intent, as-built state, commissioning state and final operational state.

LESSON 02

Progressive energisation

Understand staged introduction of energy and controlled handover at a conceptual level.

LESSON 03

Temporary configurations

Account for temporary supplies, cables, test equipment, inhibited automation and unavailable plant.

LESSON 04

Load-bank principles

Understand why artificial load is used, what system behaviour is observed and why temporary connections change the safety picture.

LESSON 05

Transfer and resilience testing

Understand the purpose of loaded transfer and resilience proving without embedding a generic switching sequence.

LESSON 06

Integrated Systems Testing

Relate electrical, UPS, transfer and mechanical responses during integrated proving.

LESSON 07

Restoration and configuration control

Verify that temporary arrangements are accounted for and the intended system state is positively re-established.

Module assessment

Micro-checks are embedded inside lessons. Development checks then test diagram recognition, energy-path tracing, explanation and application. Integrated competency is assessed later across module boundaries; completing this page does not establish competence.
