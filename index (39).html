POWERTEC TRAININGPROGRAMME
M03

HV Distribution, Switchgear, Transformers, Switching & Earthing

Translate familiar HV operational discipline into the architecture and interfaces of a data-centre electrical system.

Module method: learn → visualise → connect → apply → test → revisit in another system → demonstrate through evidence.

Learning sequence

LESSON 01

HV distribution architecture

Incoming supplies, switchboards, bus sections, transformer feeders and resilient arrangements.

LESSON 02

Switchgear families and functions

Differentiate switching, interruption, isolation, earthing and protection functions; recognise AIS, GIS and RMU concepts.

LESSON 03

Transformer interfaces

Understand transformer purpose, vector/neutral implications at a conceptual level, protection interfaces and the HV/LV operational boundary.

LESSON 04

Device state and interlocking

Interpret connected, isolated, withdrawn/test states where applicable and understand mechanical/electrical/logical interlock intent.

LESSON 05

HV/LV backfeed thinking

Challenge the assumption that removal of the normal HV source alone establishes the complete safe condition of connected equipment.

Module assessment

Micro-checks are embedded inside lessons. Development checks then test diagram recognition, energy-path tracing, explanation and application. Integrated competency is assessed later across module boundaries; completing this page does not establish competence.
