M06

ATS / STS, Source Transfer, Coupling & Synchronisation

Understand why alternative sources cannot be treated as interchangeable without considering electrical compatibility and transfer philosophy.

Module method: learn → visualise → connect → apply → test → revisit in another system → demonstrate through evidence.

Learning sequence

LESSON 01

Two sources, one load

Establish preferred and alternative source concepts.

LESSON 02

Synchronisation fundamentals

Voltage magnitude, frequency, phase sequence and phase relationship as prerequisites for controlled paralleling.

LESSON 03

Open and controlled-overlap transfer

Compare break-before-make with design-specific controlled parallel transfer.

LESSON 04

ATS operation

Understand monitored source health, transfer logic, automatic/manual states and failure modes conceptually.

LESSON 05

STS operation

Understand rapid static transfer between compatible sources and its role in critical distribution.

LESSON 06

Bus coupling

Explore how couplers alter energisation paths, fault levels, redundancy and operational boundaries.

LESSON 07

Abnormal transfer states

Incomplete transfer, failed transfer, unexpected source, inhibited automation and contradictory indication.

Module assessment

Micro-checks are embedded inside lessons. Development checks then test diagram recognition, energy-path tracing, explanation and application. Integrated competency is assessed later across module boundaries; completing this page does not establish competence.
