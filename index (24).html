POWERTEC TRAININGPROGRAMME
M10

Integrated Operational Competence & Mock Authorisation

Demonstrate whole-system reasoning across architecture, energy sources, safety, protection, resilience, commissioning and abnormal conditions.

Module method: learn → visualise → connect → apply → test → revisit in another system → demonstrate through evidence.

Learning sequence

LESSON 01

Whole-system reconstruction

Rebuild the current configuration from a fictional SLD, equipment states and operational information.

LESSON 02

Energy-source challenge

Identify normal, alternative, stored and automatic sources plus unresolved information.

LESSON 03

Operational-boundary challenge

Explain the conceptual safety boundary and challenge assumptions.

LESSON 04

Resilience and consequence

Identify lost redundancy and downstream consequences.

LESSON 05

Abnormal-event analysis

Separate known facts from assumptions and identify the next information needed.

LESSON 06

Integrated mock assessment

Multi-part assessment across knowledge, diagram interpretation, scenario judgement and evidence review.

LESSON 07

Assessment readiness

Course completion is not authorisation; readiness requires defined knowledge, scenario, evidence, mentor and AE gates.

Module assessment

Micro-checks are embedded inside lessons. Development checks then test diagram recognition, energy-path tracing, explanation and application. Integrated competency is assessed later across module boundaries; completing this page does not establish competence.
