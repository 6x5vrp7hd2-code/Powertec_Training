POWERTEC TRAININGPROGRAMME
M01

Electrical Architecture & Operational Boundaries

Build a reliable mental model of how electrical energy moves through a mission-critical facility and how operational boundaries change with configuration.

Module method: learn → visualise → connect → apply → test → revisit in another system → demonstrate through evidence.

Learning sequence

LESSON 01

Electrical quantities in operational context

Refresh voltage, current, impedance, power, apparent power, power factor, energy and frequency, then connect each directly to equipment ratings, loading, fault behaviour and source compatibility.

LESSON 02

AC, DC and three-phase systems

Re-establish AC/DC and three-phase relationships before applying them to rectifiers, DC links, batteries, inverters, phase sequence and source transfer.

LESSON 03

The data-centre power chain

Trace a generic facility from incoming supply through HV distribution, transformers, LV switchboards, resilient paths, critical power conversion and downstream loads.

LESSON 04

Reading professional SLDs

Interpret device symbols, normal states, tags, ratings, drawing continuations, bus sections, incomers, couplers and system boundaries.

LESSON 05

Architecture reconstruction

Rebuild a complex fictional SLD in layers and explain what each new layer changes about energisation, redundancy and operational control.

Module assessment

Micro-checks are embedded inside lessons. Development checks then test diagram recognition, energy-path tracing, explanation and application. Integrated competency is assessed later across module boundaries; completing this page does not establish competence.
