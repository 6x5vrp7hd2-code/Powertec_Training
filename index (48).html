POWERTEC TRAININGPROGRAMME
M05

UPS, Batteries, DC Systems & Bypass

Build UPS understanding from first principles to multi-source operational judgement.

Module method: learn → visualise → connect → apply → test → revisit in another system → demonstrate through evidence.

Learning sequence

LESSON 01

Why critical loads use UPS

Continuity, power conditioning and ride-through in mission-critical architecture.

LESSON 02

Double-conversion path

Follow AC input through rectification, DC link and inversion to the critical load.

LESSON 03

DC link and capacitors

Understand stored electrical energy, discharge behaviour and why de-energised AC does not automatically mean absence of hazardous energy.

LESSON 04

Battery as an independent source

Treat battery strings as sources with their own isolation, stored energy and DC-specific hazards.

LESSON 05

Static bypass

Understand the alternative electronic path, source compatibility and its effect on the list of credible energisation paths.

LESSON 06

Maintenance bypass

Understand why a maintenance path changes the operational architecture and can place the load outside the normal power-conversion route.

LESSON 07

UPS operating states

Recognise normal conversion, battery operation, bypass states, reduced redundancy and unavailable equipment.

LESSON 08

Parallel UPS and load sharing

Understand redundancy, parallel modules and load distribution without assuming vendor-specific control behaviour.

LESSON 09

Capacity and residual load

Use simplified capacity exercises to judge whether removing equipment consumes redundancy or leaves adequate headroom.

LESSON 10

UPS isolation reasoning

Use fictional diagrams to identify sources, stored energy, bypass paths and missing information; never substitute the exercise for an approved site procedure.

Module assessment

Micro-checks are embedded inside lessons. Development checks then test diagram recognition, energy-path tracing, explanation and application. Integrated competency is assessed later across module boundaries; completing this page does not establish competence.
