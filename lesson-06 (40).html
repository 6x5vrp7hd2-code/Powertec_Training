M05 · Lesson 06

Maintenance bypass

Understand why a maintenance path changes the operational architecture and can place the load outside the normal power-conversion route.

Why this matters

The objective is not isolated recall. The candidate needs enough first-principles understanding to recognise this subject when it reappears later inside a more complicated data-centre configuration.

Teaching sequence

Start simple. Establish the principle and vocabulary. Make it visible. Represent it with a diagram or device state. Place it in the system. Connect it to upstream and downstream equipment. Change the state. Explore what becomes different. Apply it. Use the result in a data-centre operational problem.

Data-centre application

Trace AC, DC and bypass paths independently. Treat batteries and DC-link energy as separate considerations from normal AC input.

Candidate should be able to:

Describe the subject without relying on memorised wording; recognise it on a fictional professional SLD; explain its relationship with at least one other system; identify a common unsafe assumption; and state what evidence or information would be required before a real operational decision.

Incremental check

Which approach best demonstrates understanding at this stage?

Applied exercise

Use the Architecture Lab or the module diagram pack. Highlight the relevant equipment and draw the normal energy path. Add one alternative source or abnormal state and explain what has changed.

Evidence opportunity

Where a comparable activity is encountered on site, record one O/A/D evidence item against this lesson and any other competencies demonstrated by the same activity. One practical activity may cross-credit several modules.
