POWERTEC TRAININGPROGRAMME
M09

Protection, Power Quality, Abnormal Conditions & Restoration

Connect protection and power-quality fundamentals to real operational judgement when system state differs from expectation.

Module method: learn → visualise → connect → apply → test → revisit in another system → demonstrate through evidence.

Learning sequence

LESSON 01

Prospective fault current

Understand how source impedance and configuration influence fault magnitude.

LESSON 02

Selectivity and discrimination

Understand why the nearest appropriate protective device should clear a fault while preserving healthy distribution where the design permits.

LESSON 03

Zone selective interlocking

Learn the coordination concept without assuming a universal vendor implementation.

LESSON 04

Same fault, different source

Compare the implications of transformer/mains, UPS inverter, bypass and alternate-source feeding; use approved studies/manufacturer data for actual performance.

LESSON 05

Harmonics and THD

Understand non-linear loads, current distortion and resulting voltage distortion as operational-awareness concepts.

LESSON 06

Neutral and thermal effects

Explore phase imbalance, triplen harmonics and heating as possible symptoms requiring investigation.

LESSON 07

Voltage and frequency disturbances

Recognise sags, interruptions, transients and source-quality issues.

LESSON 08

Abnormal system states

Unexpected trips, failed transfer, reduced redundancy, contradictory indication, interlock conflict, control-power failure and temporary-configuration conflict.

LESSON 09

Restoration reasoning

Reconstruct actual state, identify what changed and establish what information is needed before a controlled return to intended configuration.

Module assessment

Micro-checks are embedded inside lessons. Development checks then test diagram recognition, energy-path tracing, explanation and application. Integrated competency is assessed later across module boundaries; completing this page does not establish competence.
