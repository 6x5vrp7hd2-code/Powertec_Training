POWERTEC TRAININGPROGRAMME
Safety & Governance

Education is not operational authority.

Powertec Training develops understanding, judgement and evidence. It does not replace approved Safety Rules, OEM instructions, site procedures, switching schedules, risk controls or formal authorisation.

Core principle

Vendor- and site-neutral teaching.

Controlled layer

Site rules, procedures and drawings added only through approved local governance.

Simulation

Fictional SLDs and scenarios prevent training content being mistaken for live operating instruction.

Assessment

Programme readiness can recommend formal assessment; it never confers authorisation.

Powertec Training · Data Centre AP/SAP Development Programme · Training and assessment preparation only. Electrical authorisation remains with the appointed authorising organisation and Authorising Engineer.
